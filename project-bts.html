PROJECT 01 · BTS ELECTROTECHNICS

Cardboard Forming Machine Retrofit

Electrical retrofit of a cardboard forming machine carried out during my BTS Electrotechnics.

Project context

This project involved the electrical retrofit of a cardboard forming machine at ARO SYSTEM. The objective was to work on the machine's electrical installation and motor drive system.

My contribution

My main responsibility was the motor drive for a 1.5 kW three-phase motor supplied by a 400 V network.

  • Selection and sizing of the variable speed drive.
  • Selection of the braking resistor.
  • Study of the braking relay control.
  • Selection of the motor protection device.
  • Analysis of the existing installation and its requirements.
  • Preparation of electrical diagrams using WinRelais.

Technical skills developed

  • Electrical equipment selection and sizing.
  • Motor drive systems.
  • Reading and producing electrical diagrams.
  • Technical documentation and component specifications.
